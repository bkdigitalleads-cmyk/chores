Frequently asked questions

How often should I clean each room?
Daily for counters, dishes, and the bed; weekly for bathrooms, floors, and sheets; monthly or less for deep jobs. Chores starts every room on a schedule like the table above, and you can change any of it.

How do I make a chore chart for my family?
Add everyone who helps, assign chores or let them rotate, and print the weekly chart.

Can roommates take turns?
Yes. Turn on Take turns and the chore passes to the next person every time it's done.

Do I need an account?
No. Everything stays on your iPhone. No account, no cloud, no tracking.
