Chores: Cleaning Schedule, Support

Chores is a house cleaning schedule and chore chart for iPhone. Pick your rooms, see only what's due each day, share chores with your household, and print a weekly chart.

How do I check off a chore?

Tap the circle next to it on the Today tab or inside a room. It moves to Done today and its next date is set from today. Tapped the wrong one? Tap the check again under Done today to undo.

How do I change how often a chore repeats?

Tap the chore to open it, then pick a new frequency under How often, or tap Custom to set any number of days. You can also move its next due date with the minus and plus buttons.

How do I add a room or a chore?

Rooms tab, then Add a room or area. Starter areas (kitchen to garage, plus dog, cat, plants, yard and car) come with their usual chores. Inside a room, tap Add a chore or one of the suggested chores. On the Today tab, the plus button adds a chore to any room. For something you only need to do once, pick Just once under How often.

How do I share chores with my family or roommates?

Chart tab, then Add your household. Open any chore to assign it to someone, and turn on Take turns to rotate it between people. The chart shows who did what each week, with points.

How do I print the chore chart?

Chart tab, then Print this week's chore chart. You get a PDF with each person's chores and a box for every day they're due. Share it to a printer, email, or AirDrop.

How do I restore a purchase?

Settings, then Restore purchases. Make sure you are signed into the same Apple Account you used to buy Pro.

What does Pro cost?

Weekly $4.99 after a 3-day free trial, Yearly $29.99 after a 7-day free trial, or Lifetime $59.99 once. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Apple ID settings. Prices in USD; local pricing may vary. Free includes up to 3 rooms or areas.

Is my data private?

Yes. Everything lives only on your phone. No account, no cloud, no tracking. See the privacy policy.
